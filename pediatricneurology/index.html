Rare Genetic Disorder · Mobile App
SATB2 Family Hub
A companion app helping families track symptoms, milestones, and care routines for children with SATB2 Syndrome — a rare condition affecting speech, behavior, and skeletal development.

02
Epilepsy · Clinical Dashboard
SeizureSight — Clinician Dashboard
A web dashboard redesign for pediatric epilepsy neurologists to monitor seizure frequency, medication adherence, and EEG trends across a patient panel — reducing cognitive load during busy clinic days.
Contextual inquiry Information architecture Data visualization Accessibility
+

Pediatric epilepsy neurologists manage 20–40 patients daily, yet existing EMR dashboards bury critical data in dense tables. Through contextual inquiry at a children's hospital, I redesigned the patient panel view to surface urgent alerts, longitudinal seizure trends, and medication adherence — all above the fold.

Discover
Contextual inquiry
Shadowed 3 pediatric neurologists across 2 clinic days, noting workarounds
Define
Cognitive load audit
Mapped 14 tasks clinicians perform; identified 6 requiring 3+ clicks unnecessarily
Design
IA redesign + hi-fi
New information hierarchy, progressive disclosure, and accessible color system
Validate
Task-based testing
5 physicians; time-on-task for critical alerts cut by 62% vs. baseline

03
Rare Genetic · Onboarding Design
ARF1 Syndrome — Newly Diagnosed Family Guide
An onboarding experience for families receiving an ARF1-related neurodevelopmental diagnosis — an ultra-rare condition with fewer than 100 documented cases worldwide — designed to reduce isolation and provide immediate next steps.
Empathy mapping Content strategy Co-design Responsive web
+

When families receive an ARF1 diagnosis — a condition so rare that most clinicians have never seen a case — they face an information void. I co-designed an onboarding web experience with 4 affected families, prioritizing emotional safety alongside actionable content: who to call, what to ask the neurologist, and where to find the (tiny) global community.

Empathize
Empathy mapping
Mapped emotional states across the 72-hr window post-diagnosis with 4 families
Co-design
Family workshops
2 participatory design sessions; families sorted, named, and prioritized content
Content
Plain language audit
Rewrote 18 medical content pieces to grade 7 reading level with clinician review
Test
Tree testing + UT
Navigation success rate improved from 44% to 89% after IA restructure

About this work

My approach

Lead with research — especially in rare disease, assumptions are dangerous
Involve patients and families as co-designers, not just test participants
Measure both usability metrics and emotional impact
Accessibility is a baseline, not a feature

Skills demonstrated

User research: interviews, contextual inquiry, diary studies
Synthesis: affinity mapping, journey maps, empathy maps
Design: wireframing, prototyping, visual design in Figma
Validation: usability testing, tree testing, SUS scoring
